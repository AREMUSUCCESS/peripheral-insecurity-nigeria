# Reproducibility and research integrity

## Analysis unit

The primary analytical unit is Nigeria's 36 states plus the Federal Capital Territory (37 units).

## Main measures

- ACLED 2025 recorded events per 100,000 population.
- ACLED 2025 recorded fatalities per 100,000 population.
- NBS Multidimensional Poverty Index incidence (2021/22).
- WorldPop 2025 urban-cluster footprint as a percentage of state area.
- NBS household-reported security-related shocks (2021/22).

## Statistical approach

The analysis uses descriptive statistics, Pearson and Spearman correlations, multiple linear regression, regression diagnostics, influence diagnostics, multicollinearity checks, and sensitivity analyses.

No causal inference is attempted.

## Sensitivity analysis

Borno is retained in the main analysis despite its influence on some regression diagnostics. A separate sensitivity analysis excludes Borno. A further robustness analysis excludes both Borno and Zamfara.

## Important measurement limitations

The datasets differ in reference period and measurement system. ACLED records reported events and fatalities; NBS household data measure reported household security-related shocks. WorldPop urban-cluster footprint is a geographical area measure rather than an urban population share. ACLED rates use 2022 population projections as denominators for 2025 recorded events and fatalities.

These materials should not be interpreted as evidence of causal relationships.
